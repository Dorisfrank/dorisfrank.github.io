08. ERP Market Research and Evaluation

Executive Overview

Given what Sections 01 through 07 established IntelliMed actually needs, which ERP platforms genuinely fit?

Everything before this section was discovery. This one is where that discovery gets tested against what the market actually offers. I researched four ERP platforms with a genuine, active presence in hospital and health system deployments, not a generic enterprise ERP list, and evaluated each against the five criteria the brief specifies: cost, compliance support, scalability, usability, and implementation timeline.

I did not evaluate these platforms against generic ERP criteria. I evaluated them against IntelliMed's own requirements, three Must Have compliance requirements, an integration gap as the dominant finding across Section 05, and a mid-sized three-location network rather than a large multinational health system.

Platforms Evaluated

Four platforms with an established presence in hospital ERP deployments, drawn from current market research on healthcare-specific ERP adoption:

  • SAP S/4HANA for Healthcare
  • Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP
  • Infor CloudSuite Healthcare
  • Microsoft Dynamics 365

Evaluation Methodology

Each platform was evaluated against the criteria defined in the project brief, and validated against IntelliMed's own business priorities established during discovery. Assessment focused on:

  • Alignment with functional requirements documented in Section 03
  • Support for non-functional requirements, particularly security and compliance
  • Ability to address the integration gaps identified as dominant in Section 05
  • Suitability for a three-location hospital network specifically, not enterprise scale generally
  • Estimated implementation effort, scalability, and total cost of ownership

Evaluation Summary

Two findings stood out to me against IntelliMed's specific profile, not just the general market.

Compliance depth and implementation timeline trade against each other. Infor CloudSuite Healthcare offers the strongest native, healthcare-specific compliance functionality of the four, directly relevant given NFR-01, NFR-02, and NFR-03 are all rated Must Have. But its implementation timeline, up to 30 months at the healthcare-specific end, is the longest of any platform evaluated, a real cost against a three-location network that needs the fragmentation problem solved, not managed for another two and a half years.

Scale fit matters more than platform reputation. SAP S/4HANA and Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP are widely adopted enterprise ERP platforms with strong capabilities for large, complex organizations, but both are built for, and priced for, large multi-entity organizations. IntelliMed is a three-location network. Unless significant expansion is already planned, both platforms likely represent more cost and complexity than IntelliMed's actual scale requires, this is a case where the most recognized vendor name is not automatically the best fit.

Microsoft Dynamics 365 is the fastest and least expensive of the four, but its compliance support is explicitly described as requiring deliberate configuration rather than being native out of the box, meaning IntelliMed's Compliance Officer would need direct involvement from day one of implementation, not just at go-live, to avoid the exact audit-trail gap identified in Section 05.

Consultant's Observation: I am not recommending a single platform. The evidence does not support one, each of the four carries a genuine trade-off against IntelliMed's specific priorities, and picking a winner without a real budget and timeline constraint, which Section 07 explicitly deferred to the business case phase, would be recommending a number I don't have the authority to set. What I can say with confidence: Infor CloudSuite Healthcare and Microsoft Dynamics 365 both merit a closer, vendor-led evaluation given IntelliMed's mid-sized scale, while SAP S/4HANA and Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP should only advance if IntelliMed's expansion plans extend meaningfully beyond three locations.

Executive Close

What should leadership remember before moving on?

Executive Takeaway

The objective of ERP evaluation is not to identify the most powerful platform.

It is to identify the platform whose capabilities, implementation effort, and long-term operating model best align with IntelliMed's actual business objectives.

I researched four platforms and compared them against IntelliMed's own requirements, not generic ERP criteria. This evaluation narrows the market to the solutions most aligned with IntelliMed's documented business requirements, Infor CloudSuite Healthcare and Microsoft Dynamics 365 merit direct vendor evaluation given IntelliMed's current scale.

Final vendor selection should proceed through demonstrations, proof-of-concept workshops, commercial negotiations, and reference checks before contract award. The next and final section compiles every deliverable from this engagement into the submission leadership actually reviews.
