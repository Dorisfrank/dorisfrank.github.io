# EcoGrow Delivery Evidence

## Purpose

This folder holds the supporting delivery evidence referenced across the EcoGrow project documentation, Jira work items, validation records, and final assurance review.

I built this archive to preserve a traceable connection between delivery claims and the underlying prototype, acceptance-testing, and usage-event evidence, so that a claim in Section 7 or the RTM can always be traced back to the real file that supports it, rather than trusted on the strength of a sentence alone.

This folder does not create new delivery evidence and does not replace the governing project artefacts. It archives evidence captured during EcoGrow's real delivery and validation activities.

---

## Evidence Principles

I am holding this archive to the same principles that have governed the rest of the project:

- No fabricated or reconstructed evidence.
- Evidence is retained only where it exists in the verified source set.
- Jira workflow status is not treated as proof of formal product acceptance.
- Prototype and acceptance-testing evidence are kept distinct from formal MVP acceptance.
- Product Owner Persona review outcomes are not represented as formal all-persona Sprint Review evidence.
- Release or version assignment is not treated as release approval.
- Pre-operational reporting and framework measures are not represented as realised operational benefits.
- Open governance gates remain visible and are not quietly resolved by omission.
- Evidence claims are not expanded beyond what the underlying file can actually demonstrate.

## Folder Structure

### Prototype Walkthrough Evidence

Contains screenshots and video evidence captured during prototype walkthroughs and acceptance testing for the seven MVP stories. Each story folder is organised by its Jira issue key and the functional requirement it supports.

### ECO-10 Evidence Treatment

ECO-10 / FR-08 has two evidence classifications, deliberately kept separate:

- **Canonical:** the prototype evidence covering Plant Library and Harvest Tracker's planting and harvest actions, representing two of the three required capabilities.
- **Supplementary Community Forum:** the evidence covering the third capability.

I am preserving this split so the two evidence sets are never treated as interchangeable proof of the same thing. Together they support coverage of FR-08's required capability set; individually, neither should be represented as complete evidence for all three capabilities.

### Usage Event Evidence

Contains evidence supporting usage-event validation where it applies. I have kept this separate from functional acceptance evidence because a prototype working successfully does not itself prove operational analytics or real production usage.

## Evidence Status

This archive supports story-level prototype and acceptance-testing claims where real evidence exists. On its own, it does not establish:

- Formal all-persona Sprint Review completion.
- Final MVP or product acceptance.
- Release approval.
- Production usage.
- Realised business benefits.

Those distinctions remain governed by the EcoGrow validation, release-readiness, and final-assurance documentation, not by this folder.

## Stakeholder and Governance Boundary

This evidence archive does not amend or replace the governing stakeholder documentation. Any potential stakeholder coverage gap identified during final assurance review, including end-user, support, or compliance considerations, must be revalidated against the actual Stakeholder Register, Power-Interest Grid, RACI, engagement approach, and relevant project sections before Section 1 is reopened or amended.

I will not retrospectively add stakeholder entries simply because a general framework suggests they should exist. Any controlled amendment must be supported by the real project record and must not imply historical participation or engagement that did not occur.

The Gardener (STK-07) and Support (STK-08) additions already made to Section 1 are not examples of this concern. Those were added following a documented gap analysis against the existing register, not asserted from a general framework alone, and they stand as verified, completed governance work.
