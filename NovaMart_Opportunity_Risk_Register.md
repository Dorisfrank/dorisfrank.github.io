## RISK REGISTER

| # | Risk | Description | Impact | Probability | Risk Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Pending revenue ageing into uncollectable status | ₦22.6M in pending payments. Without a retry mechanism, pending orders age and become progressively harder to collect | HIGH — ₦22.6M cash flow exposure | HIGH — no current retry process in place | CRITICAL | Implement automated payment retry at 24h / 48h / 72h intervals within 30 days |
| R2 | Bundle G customer trust damage | 1,534 customers paid for a product officially listed as inactive. If the deactivation was intentional and customers are not informed, this creates a customer trust and potential refund risk | MEDIUM — brand and retention | MEDIUM — currently unresolved | HIGH | Audit and resolve Bundle G status within 7 days; communicate with affected customers |
| R3 | Revenue plateau without intervention | Monthly confirmed revenue has stabilised between ₦15.3M and ₦17.4M with no growth trend over 10 months. Without conversion or acquisition activity, NovaMart's revenue will not grow | HIGH — long-term growth risk | HIGH — trend already visible | HIGH | Implement one-time to recurring conversion campaign; review bundle expansion strategy |
| R4 | Payment failure rate above industry benchmark | All three payment methods exceed the 2–3% digital commerce benchmark. At current failure rates, NovaMart loses approximately ₦13M per year in payment failures | HIGH — ₦13M annual revenue loss | HIGH — systemic across all methods | HIGH | Engage payment processor for failure log review; focus on Bank Transfer (5.4% rate) first |
| R5 | Loyalty programme misalignment | Loyalty tiers do not reflect customer spending behaviour. Platinum customers spend the same per order as Silver customers. Without redesign, the programme cannot be used to identify or protect high-value relationships | MEDIUM — retention effectiveness | MEDIUM — ongoing by design | MEDIUM | Redesign tier criteria with spend-based thresholds within 90 days |
| R6 | Discount erosion without volume offset | Orders at 21–25% discount generate 18.5% lower avg value. If volume does not compensate, these discounts are eroding margin without commercial justification. Note: full margin impact requires cost data | MEDIUM — revenue per order | MEDIUM — pattern visible in data | MEDIUM | A/B test discount levels; set evidence-based ceiling based on conversion data |
| R7 | Abuja market underperformance | Abuja trails every other city on revenue, volume, and avg order value. Without investigation, the root cause (product-market fit, logistics, awareness) remains unknown and the gap persists | MEDIUM — geographic revenue concentration | LOW–MEDIUM — slow drift | MEDIUM | Commission a structured market investigation in Month 3 of the roadmap |
| R8 | Systemic support quality failure | No issue type achieves an avg rating above 3.71. Escalation rates of ~10% are double the expected benchmark. Platinum customers — highest retention priority — have the lowest satisfaction | HIGH — churn and brand risk | HIGH — systemic across all categories | HIGH | Implement 4.0 avg rating target with monthly tracking; conduct escalation root cause review |
| R9 | Product Quality complaints driving escalations | Product Quality has the lowest rating (3.55) and the highest escalation rate (10.2%). If not addressed at the supplier level, this will continue to generate the most serious customer complaints | MEDIUM — product and brand risk | HIGH — already the top escalation type | HIGH | Supplier quality review and bundle curation audit within 60 days |
| R10 | Data model integrity (Bundle G) | The presence of active orders against an inactive bundle indicates a gap between the product catalogue and the ordering system. If this can happen for Bundle G, it may be possible for other products | MEDIUM — data reliability | LOW — currently one instance | MEDIUM | Conduct full product catalogue integrity audit; implement system validation preventing orders against inactive bundles |
