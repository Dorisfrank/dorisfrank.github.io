# EcoGrow Portfolio Master Index

**Prepared by:** Doris Frank, Business Analyst
**Purpose:** Single navigation point from the EcoGrow engagement story to every supporting artifact, split by phase, so a reader can go as deep as they want without losing the thread.

## 1. How to Read This Project

EcoGrow is one engagement told in two phases:

- **Phase 1, MVP Delivery**: a real, evidenced BI/BA engagement for GreenSphere Innovations. Plant Library, Harvest Tracker, and Community Forum, built, tracked in Jira, and governed through a full stakeholder, RACI, and traceability structure.
- **Phase 2, Future-State Discovery**: six candidate epics (F01 through F06) taken through a disciplined discovery, translation, QA, and packaging sequence. This phase is analysis and requirements-engineering work only. Nothing in Phase 2 was built, approved, or committed to Jira.

I keep these two phases visually and structurally separate everywhere this project appears, including here, so nobody mistakes discovery work for delivered work.
